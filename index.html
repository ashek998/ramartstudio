About RAM Art Studio

Artworks made for scale, celebration, and lasting impact.

Led by Vishwas BJ, RAM Art Studio works across traditional and contemporary sculptural forms. The studio handles concept development, clay modelling, moulding, fibreglass finishing, and installation-ready artworks with careful attention to proportion, expression, surface, and site.

From idols and statues to decorative event pieces and public-space installations, every project is treated as a commissioned artwork with its own story, scale, and finish.
