Our story

Tradition in the hand.
Possibility in every form.

RAM Art Studio, led by Vishwas BJ, creates handcrafted sculpture across clay modelling, moulding, fibreglass finishing and installation work.

Each commission begins with its purpose and place. Proportion, expression, surface and finish are developed around the artwork being made.

Vishwas BJ

FOUNDER · SCULPTOR

Vishwas BJ leads RAM Art Studio’s sculptural practice, working from concept and clay modelling through moulding, fibreglass finishing and installation-ready artwork.

The studio’s work moves between devotional sculpture, custom forms and spatial installations, with close attention to expression, detail and finish.
